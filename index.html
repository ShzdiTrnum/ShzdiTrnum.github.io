Turrnum Shahzadi

Ph.D. Candidate · Tokyo Metropolitan University

Turrnum Shahzadi is a 3rd year Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Tokyo Metropolitan University (東京都立大学) in Tokyo, Japan, advised by Prof. Norio Tagawa. She completed her M.S. and M.Sc. in Information Technology at the University of Gujrat in Pakistan, where she later worked as a lecturer.

Her research interests include medical image and video analysis, computer vision, and probabilistic machine learning. Her background spans fasciculation detection in ALS muscle ultrasound, MRI-based spine segmentation and classification, motion analysis, and clinical video annotation.

If you're interested in collaborating, feel free to reach her at turrnum@gmail.com.
