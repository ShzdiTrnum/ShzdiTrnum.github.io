Turrnum Shahzadi

Ph.D. Candidate · Tokyo Metropolitan University

I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Tokyo Metropolitan University, advised by Prof. Norio Tagawa. My research develops deep learning and probabilistic methods for medical image and video analysis.

My doctoral work detects fasciculations, a diagnostic marker of ALS, in muscle ultrasound video using black-box variational inference and deep generative modelling, in clinical collaboration with Dr. Kota Bokuda at Tokyo Metropolitan Neurological Hospital. I have also worked on MRI-based lumbar spine analysis and on building annotated clinical video datasets.

Before my Ph.D., I was a lecturer at the University of Gujrat, Pakistan, where I taught programming and supervised student ML/DL projects.
